### Visitor Comments

**Jimmy Cowie** (2007-12-12)

> Apart from Harry Hall, now living in the old seal house, the house next to the bridge Maxwell the seal hunter used to live there. We used to have lots of fun on the old fishing boat that was across from the seal house.My brother Bobby Willie Turner and his brother Chris, John Pate, Bunter Robertson, Rab Sheppard and a few more had great times as bairns playing around the old and new harbour

**Constance** (2008-06-20)

> Who remembers swimming here?
> And it was OK with my Mum????

**William Turner** (2008-11-28)

> It was just as jimmy says... wild boys playing and getting into bother... Bobby was my best pal and jimmy was his older brother..Old days and old memories..pirates, cowboys, bonfires,spears and bike riding on the braes.
