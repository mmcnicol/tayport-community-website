**Old**

Unlike the front, the back view of Tayport School has changed completely. From very early on it became clear that space was at a premium, and as the twentieth century progressed accommodation became a real problem... The problem was solved by various extensions and alterations, in particular that large 1936 extension which covers the entire back face of the building.
