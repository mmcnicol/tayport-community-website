### Visitor Comments

**Bill Macdonald in Australia.** (2005-07-13)

> The white house on the left was my home. from 1935 til 1955. Thanks Andy, its first time Ive seen it since it was reconstructed.

**neil** (2006-05-30)

> I think the house just visible on the left with the railings is where my aunt, Molly Harris, lived until she died in the early 1950's. I think she lived in the lower flat and I recall being there when very young and seeing from her window the Fifie (Tay Ferries) making a crossing.

**kate graham** (2009-03-09)

> My brother and i used to love going down this hill on our homemade piler

**Derek Roger** (2010-03-07)

> The shop on the bottom left hand corner was my great uncle Alec Roger's grocery store long ago . In the mid 50s I used to help deliver groceries from the same shop but I cant remember who owned it at that time .

**Derek Roger** (2010-09-29)

> The shop at the bottom left was a grocers . It was owned by my late fathers uncle Alec Roger , It was Thompsons as I recollect in the mid 50's .
> I used to help deiver messages and pick up orders on a Saturday helping Lyle Croal who drove the message bike . A big heavy thing with an oversized basket on the front . After deliveries we had to bike to Newport to the chicken farm which was opposite Rogers wood and pick up a large order of eggs for the stores weekly supply .
