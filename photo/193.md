> I suppose I am one of the few who have been to the top of the lighthouse . It would have been in the mid 1950's .

> She had a small boat from which she fished ; I was not allowed on it ( her fathers rule )  I fished from the sand banks  for flukes or from the Reevet Rock at high tide .
>  Always had a fire going and cooked  welks  for a snck while fishing .
>
> Oh Happy days .
