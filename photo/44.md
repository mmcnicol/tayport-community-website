### Visitor Comments

**Doreen Smith (McDonald) Woodbridge,  Ont** (2006-12-20)

> I used to live in number 2. We were one of the original families (McDonalds). What a great place to grow up. We played in the "circle" till the street lights went on or our mothers called from the window announcing that "dinner was ready!". There were no cars parked in the circle, the only family with a car was the Patties (#1). I remember the Ritchies, Cooks, Oswalds, Redpaths, Aulds, Edwards, Lawsons, Taianos, Fitchets, Jacks & the Mackays. The only family with a phone was the Oswalds. Thanks for the photograph Andy - great memories.

**Sheena Liddell (munro)** (2007-03-12)

> I lived at number 21 1953-1960, next door to the Cooks and downstairs from the Aulds: a great place to grow up!
