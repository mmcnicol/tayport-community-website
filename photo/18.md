**Derek Roger** (2008-12-09)

> In the 50's I also helped Wull doctor from time to time  ; going up to the houses and taking orders then delivering the produce . We always had to ask if there was any old bread for the horse !
> On my first occasion when I got some bread I gave it to the horse . What are you doing he bellowed ?  The bread is to feed the chickens  !!
> At that time I lived on Craig Road  and after Wull had passed I was sent out by my mother to shovel up the dung if we were lucky which went on the rhubarb .
