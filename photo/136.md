We are hoping to visit there later in the year.  Would like to know how much it has changed since then.  We lived in a house on  Commonty Road (I think that was the name. **Irene Bowman** (2010-03-25)

> The thieves nicked our car from Elizabeth St. to transport the loot to Tentsmuir, where they forced open a small safe they had taken, only to find bags of coins which they dumped.   Brian Wyles, one of the tellers, complained of green fingers from counting all the coppers.   The car was found in Glasgow by the Police.   
> Had a great afternoon going through all the photographs - wonderful to recognize so many names such as Catherine Jaraszkiewicz, Marjorie Duff, Robert Gourlay and Greta Gaul - aah, where have the years gone?

**Bruce Scott** (2011-04-07)
