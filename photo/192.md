### Visitor Comments

**John A Miller** (2005-02-10)

> This house was the old Police Station. I lived there from 08/09/1955 until 09/01/1958 when my father was village policeman.

**Constance** (2006-05-26)

> I lived next door at 64, from 1939 to 1951

**Rich** (2006-06-02)

> I knew a man who lived here. I was very young, so it was at least between '79 and '86ish. His  name was Gib, sorry, I only knew his first name. I remember him being very kind, he was in TAMS and I remember him playing Fagin in a production of Oliver! Very scary stuff.
> He died of cancer, I was too young to really understand what it meant but, as far as I'm concerned, that picture is of Gib's house.

**doug dear** (2006-10-16)

> we lived in this house from about 1972 until Gibb Watson bought it to allow us to move to 'Glenogle' in 1986 where we have stayed since - Gibb was a kindly person and was very understanding over the house transaction - originally the house had been owned by Rick Kennedy and his wife who had done some renovation - we had to demolish the old Police Cells and dispose of the huge steel doors which George Birrell and his crew collected - with difficulty - this was a cosy and compact family home for us where be started our family at the time of 2 boys - we had great neighbours in the Pattersons and the Hills - it was a happy time with good memories

**Shona Allen** (2008-01-10)

> Gibb Watson was my father.  We moved to 60 Castle Street in the Autumn of 1980.  I'm glad that people have good memories of Dad.  I have a photograph of him as Fagin, Rich.  Scary stuff indeed, but he was as Doug says, a kindly and very understanding man.  My sister brings her children to Tayport for holidays and was at the house this Christmas.  We both have very fond memories of living there.

**munro985@btinternet.com** (2008-06-19)

> I lived in here from 50/55, m father was local policeman. attended local j.s.school.started work with local firm G.P.FINDLAY as radio/tv engr.happy days in that town.
