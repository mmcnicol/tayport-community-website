### Visitor Comments

**G.** (2007-01-12)

> what a dreary looking street, but i bet it had a lot of character!

**Constance** (2007-04-23)

> It sure did have character, I lived right next door to the police station!
> Never knew it to be dreary though. Always somebody to stop and chat with.

**Mary Spain.** (2008-06-09)

> My shop is also in this picture. It hasn't changed much except most of the shops are not there now including mine[now houses], but still a very friendly street always someone you know to stop and chat.

**William turner** (2008-11-28)

> My house was all the way down on the right at No 47 opposite Wullie Fairlies barber shop..

**Catherine Jaraszkiewicz** (2009-05-01)

> Willie Fairlie, the barber, used to star in the Tayport variety concerts, playing tunes on a saw- no kidding! The sound was a bit like a violin. Tayport had talent!
