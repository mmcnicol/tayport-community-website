**Old · Fire Brigade**

Tayport Fire Station Crew. On top of the fire engine, the wee boy ( now nearing retirement age )  Brian Wyles ( lives in Newport ), sub officer Willie Kemp the local joiner, George Mitchell the coal man, George Berry, James Weir, Tam Bryson the painter and the last one I'm not too sure about. Photo - thanks to a relation of James Weir, and Graeme Kirkwood.
