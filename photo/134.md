### Visitor Comments

**Tina** (2007-09-17)

> It would be interesting to know whether the tombstones have ever been photographed and put on-line for those tracing their family ancestry? I have an interest in the family of Charles Ramsay who was a Parochial Schoolmaster, 1800 - c1845, son of Flax Dresser James Ramsay and Margaret Guthrie, and Catherine Berrie, the daughter of Ship's Master, Andrew Berrie and Agnes Berwick. They had nine children altogether, between 1827 and 1845, several of which are proving difficult to trace.

**FionaC** (2010-09-29)

> Hi Tina
> I may be three years too late for your enquiry but it seems that one daughter of Charles Ramsay the schoolmaster (Mary - born 22/06/1830)married my Great Great Great Grandfather James Wallace (it was his second marriage) in Dundee in 1873. They eventually retired to Tayport and both died there - James in 1900 and Mary of double pneumonia on 8th Nov 1901. Her brother John resgistered her death.
