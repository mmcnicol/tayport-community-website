**Rosalinde Wright nee Roger** (2009-08-29)

> Childhood memories of Castle Street.My Aunt Jean was an Auchterlonie the green grocers. She married Ernest Roger of Whinbrae. They had one son, Alistair, and they emigrated to New Zealand.As a child I used to spend many hours playing shop in the Miss Martin's hat shop next door to the Auchterlonies. Also in Castle Street was Mr Gunn,the butcher. I also remember fondly, Johnny Gibb the baker.
