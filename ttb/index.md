## Tayport Talks Back

<img src="https://tayport.org.uk/ttb/TTBposterNewSHMax800pxBanner.jpg" alt="Tayport Talks Back webpage banner" width="541" height="385">

Tayport Talks Back is a community project to help us shape the future of Tayport by bringing people together to create a Local Place Plan.

The Plan will look at what Tayport is like now and what we want it to be like in the future. Download our one-page explainer [here](https://tayport.org.uk/ttb/Talks Back Explainerv1.018Oct2026.pdf) 

The Plan is being developed by Tayport Community Council who have formed a Steering Group to develop the plan. They will ask everyone in the community to give their views and ideas. 

This page is your one-stop shop for the next few months….come back here whenever you want to find out what is happening and track the progress of the Plan.

If you’d like to get in touch with the Steering Group, please email us at ttb@gmail.com.

## Share your 3 wishes for a better community 

The first step in Tayport Talks Back is the survey asking for your 3 Wishes for Tayport. 

We would love to hear **THREE wishes you have for Tayport**. They can be big, small, practical or ambitious

Share your wishes by completing the online form [here](https://forms.gle/FmVGKz1kfMiU3RUD7) (**EASIEST OPTION!**). 

You can also:
* Complete a 3 wishes postcard available at select places across Tayport.
* Sharing your 3 wishes with us at Tayport Talks Back stall at community events over the next couple of months. 

**The survey closes on 31 December 2026.**
