## David Dodds

*Memories*

email gdoddsca at yahoo.ca

Hi: I have just discovered your web page on Tayport. I have really enjoyed it as has brought back my childhood memories. I noticed some class pics from the fifties in your historical section and thought you might like one more. This one was from either 1955-56 or 1956-57.

I left for Canada in the summer of 1957. I have visited Tayport twice since leaving once in 1972 and once in 1990 with my family. I have some slides & pics from both trips which bring back fond recollections. I noticed quite a few changes but Tayport still maintained its charm.

I look forward to more news on your website and maybe another visit to Tayport itself. In the picture I am in the top row of the far left side, beside me is John Dalrymple. I can remember other childrens names but cannot positively identify them. Maybe some of your readers will notice themselves. Click [here](/photo/227&theme=) to see the photo.

Well I better go now and hope you can use the pic. I look forward to keeping up with your website - keep up the good work.
